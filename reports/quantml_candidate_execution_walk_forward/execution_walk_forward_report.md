# Candidate execution walk-forward

## Scope
This run tests three previously shortlisted candidates with run-level walk-forward validation and stricter execution assumptions.

## Candidates
- `classic_early_drop_down`: Buy Down when BTC is down at least 20 dollars by minute 1; execution filters use only signal/fill quotes.
- `btc2_path_eff`: (btc_move_2m / 50) * path_efficiency_first2m; high buys Up, low buys Down.
- `size_imbalance_uncertainty`: size_imbalance_updown_2m * uncertainty; high buys Up, low buys Down.

## Execution assumptions
- Signal uses the 1m or 2m snapshot, then fills at the first later quote after a 4s or 8s delay.
- A fill is rejected if the delayed quote moves against the signal by more than the selected limit.
- The selected fill quote receives an added adverse slippage of 0.25c or 0.75c.
- Fill must pass spread, overround, depth, and price bounds at the delayed quote.
- Only 25% of visible size is treated as fillable, with 1% or 2% bankroll sizing.
- Max 10 trades per rolling 144-event window; after two losses, skip the next six events.
