# Quality-first research: public sources and reproducible tests

Source review date: **2026-10-15**; supplemental public-repository review: **2026-10-21**. This document records public research mechanisms and evaluation contracts. It contains no private experiment results, tuned strategy parameters, live-order instructions, or claim of profitability. The intended next stage is historical replay followed by observation-only forward testing.

## 1. Verify the instrument before training

- **Resolution source:** the official BTC five-minute market titled October 14, 2026, 9:20–9:25 PM ET names the **Chainlink BTC/USD TWAP 60-second stream**. Save each market's original rules, source URL, outcome identities, and rule hash. Do not substitute exchange candles or assume an instantaneous oracle close. The source name alone does not establish precisely which boundary observations the venue uses; verify labels against the final official outcome. [Official market and rules](https://polymarket.com/event/btc-updown-5m-1792027200).
- **Fees:** the current official fee page gives crypto taker fees as `shares * 0.07 * price * (1 - price)` and zero maker fees. This is a documentation snapshot, not a universal historical constant. Archive each market's fee configuration, including rate, exponent, role and effective time; use the applicable configuration in replay. [Fees](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees), [market fee fields](https://docs.polymarket.com/market-data/market-details), accessed 2026-10-15.
- **Minimum-size units:** the order-placement page describes `/book.min_order_size` as shares, while the market-details page describes Gamma `orderMinSize` as USDC notional. Official SDK issue #302, opened **2026-09-11**, documents this discrepancy and remains open at review. Store raw value, endpoint, protocol version and explicit unit separately. An unknown unit means order eligibility is unknown; never silently interpret a bare value as dollars or shares. [Order-placement contract](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/place-orders), [market-details contract](https://docs.polymarket.com/market-data/market-details), [SDK issue #302](https://github.com/Polymarket/py-sdk/issues/302).
- **Protocol identity:** current documentation distinguishes CTF token IDs and Protocol V2 position IDs. Select identifiers using the actual market version; documentation supporting V2 does not establish that a particular market has migrated. Reject unknown versions for executable simulation. [Market identifiers](https://docs.polymarket.com/market-data/market-details), accessed 2026-10-15.
- **Cash timing:** maker rebates are distributed daily with a minimum accrued payout of $1 according to current documentation. Do not add an unpaid rebate to immediately reusable cash. Track matching, settlement, redemption and cash availability separately. [Maker rebates](https://docs.polymarket.com/programs/maker-rebates), accessed 2026-10-15.

Historical data from different oracle, fee or protocol regimes must remain identifiable. Migration of a collector does not retroactively change historical rules. The linked Chainlink stream page returned HTTP 403 during this review, so the exact TWAP boundary calculation was **not independently established** from Chainlink documentation.

## 2. Keep only data fit for the hypothesis

Use contiguous, causally ordered segments with verified identities and target labels. Keep an intact segment even when another part of its market window is missing; exclude corrupt, duplicate, stale or incompatible observations without interpolating executable quotes. A training feature and its later target must belong to the same accepted segment. For execution claims require genuine quotes and depth for both outcomes, valid prices, source timestamps, collector receive timestamps and the applicable trading constraints. Retain an exclusion manifest and counts by date so the selected population is reproducible.

Determine quality eligibility without looking at realized strategy profit. Separate prediction features from later settlement labels. Split by whole market and chronological blocks, keeping each market entirely within one partition. Freeze model selection before consuming the forward period. Reporting thousands of snapshots from a few markets as thousands of independent outcomes is invalid.

## 3. Four bounded experiments

| Priority | Public mechanism | Reproducible test and falsification condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Measuring Trade Direction in a Prediction Market, arXiv:2610.06412v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06412v1), submitted **2026-10-05**; [author replication package](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23164515) | Match public prints to the taker settlement leg before constructing trade-flow features; distinguish receipt-time accounting from event-time joins. Trade-sign errors and weighting can change measured trading-cost conclusions. | Its selected-day evidence does not identify causal price impact or an executable five-minute strategy. Maker/taker legs must not be pooled as independent prints. |
| 1 | **Market probability plus a residual.** Treat the order-book probability as the baseline; test whether oracle-relative distance, time remaining, volatility and observable book/flow state add useful information. | Fit a simple regularized conditional model, then compare with the unmodified book on untouched chronological data. Report probability calibration and net executable payoff. Stop expanding the model when apparent improvement disappears after ask-side entry, current fees and measured delay, or depends on a single date. |
| 2 | **Same-market complete sets.** A matched unit of each mutually exclusive, exhaustive outcome has a combined payout of one unit of collateral under its actual resolution contract. | Observe two genuine synchronized books. Require executable quantities and total cost below the applicable combined payout after both legs' costs. Replay legs independently, including unmatched inventory and cash lockup. Reject opportunities that disappear after receive-time synchronization or realistic delay. Never synthesize the second book as `1 - first_price`. |
| 3 | **Receive-time information lag.** An external feed or oracle update may precede an executable quote adjustment. | Record both source event time and collector receive time. Make decisions only from messages received before decision time; replay using measured collection and execution delay. Reject a result that exists only with event-time alignment, retrospective nearest-tick joins or unavailable future oracle observations. |
| 4 | **Conditional passive liquidity.** Test whether spread capture remains positive after conditioning on subsequent unfavorable price movement and inventory. | A quote touching the market is not a fill. Require sufficient subsequent opposing trade volume after accounting for queue ahead and cancellation delay; report conservative fill bounds when queue state is unobservable. Evaluate without assumed rebates first. Without quote/trade sequence evidence, classify the experiment as unidentifiable rather than profitable. |

For a small account, rank eligible candidates using net account growth, cash-reuse time, attainable order size, opportunity frequency and terminal balance distributions. Also report how often the account falls below the venue's minimum executable amount. A fresh five-minute market is an observation opportunity, not proof that settled cash can be compounded every five minutes. A 100-fold balance target is a simulation outcome to measure, not an assumed return or a model-selection guarantee.

Ten prospective paper fills can exercise a frozen pipeline, but cannot alone establish a stable economic advantage. Keep engineering readiness, sample sufficiency and economic evidence as separate outputs.

**Canary counting rule.** A Canary is exactly ten chronological, owner-scoped fills on one evolving cash ledger. Row *n + 1* may use only cash released and reconciled after row *n*; a reset, concatenated windows, independent starting balances or paper-only fill makes the chain non-admissible. The first ten rows must carry the entry, fee/lock, settlement, release and post-release cash checkpoints, so the compounding path can be audited row by row.

**Small-capital floor.** Under the current crypto taker contract, a $5 minimum entry from a $10 ledger incurs a positive fee, so the immediately available balance is below the next $5 minimum. If that first position settles at zero, the next entry cannot be funded. A ten-row taker chain therefore has to survive its first loss under a separately verified cash rule; otherwise the capital-compatible route is passive liquidity, which still requires real queue, fill, settlement and cash-release evidence.

## 5. Preserve the authenticated PolyBolt source boundary

The current authenticated crypto reference stream is documented as `prices.crypto.twap` with the canonical symbol `btcusd` and a 60-second lookback. Subscribe frames may contain a bounded history snapshot; update frames carry a decimal value, event timestamp, sequence number and optional dropped count. The value is already decimal and must not be E18-scaled. [Real-time TWAP contract](https://docs.polymarket.com/market-data/realtime-data#twap-prices), [RTDS-to-PolyBolt migration](https://docs.polymarket.com/migrate/rtds-to-polybolt), reviewed 2026-10-18.

The legacy settlement audit remains intentionally pinned to its legacy schema. The independent future-only contract in `analysis/polybolt_twap_adapter.py` requires explicit authentication, receive time, connection and sequence scope, raw payload hash, `seq`, an explicit zero `dropped` count, strict millisecond timestamps and exact decimal strings. Its continuity manifest rejects gaps, duplicates, reordering and implicit cross-reconnect joins. A valid source stream still does not establish an order fill, settlement, redemption or reusable-cash event; those are separate gates.

For a future account-level admission, `analysis/validate_polybolt_compound_bundle.py` accepts only schema v2: exactly ten events on one declared `ledger_id`/`chain_id`, linked sequence and balance checkpoints, numeric cash arithmetic and release receipts, a per-fill source capture/sequence/hash that arrived before entry, one distinct pre-entry CLOB depth snapshot per fill, and one independently captured settlement receipt per fill. The depth snapshot must carry a payload hash, tick-valid ask and enough available shares for the executed quantity; the settlement receipt must carry a payload hash and a timestamp/payout linked to the event and cash release. Account rows must be confirmed, cash-reconciled and independently checkpointed. The validator rejects concatenated independent ledgers, lucky-prefix count changes, pre-window or post-entry source references, stale or insufficient depth, unconfirmed evidence and mismatched settlement or balance amounts. This boundary is synthetic-testable now, but it does not create a receipt bundle or change the current zero-Canary state.

The H4 settlement-grounded whale-flow boundary also requires an explicit boolean `settlement_reverted=false` beside `settlement_status=CONFIRMED`. A matched order or public print is not final merely because it exists in an off-chain feed: the settlement receipt must show that the on-chain execution was not reverted before its payout and cash-release evidence can be considered. This field was added after reviewing the 2026 study of off-chain matches and on-chain reverts; it is a source-contract gate, not a strategy result.

These are checks of supplied claims and their internal consistency. Authentication flags, checkpoint identifiers and syntactically valid hashes do not establish independent origin. The offline validator now reports `offline_consistency_passed`; `fills` and the legacy `*_verified` detail flags remain diagnostics within `evidence_scope=offline_bundle_consistency_only`. It always reports `gate_passed=false`, `receipt_authenticity_verified=false`, `actual_fills=0` and `verified_canary_fills=0`. The validator CLI exits zero for consistent input; the private runner exits zero whenever it writes a diagnostic record, including rejected input. Neither exit code authorizes admission. The workflow seals an explicit `polybolt-compound-offline-consistency-sealed-v1` report, keeps any passing row count under `offline_consistent_compounding_rows`, and never promotes it to verified canary fills or actual same-ledger compounding rows. No independent receipt-authentication mechanism is implemented by this path.
