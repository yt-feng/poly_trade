# Sealed source coverage audit

The manual `sealed-source-coverage` workflow audits registered public snapshot and label archives without training a strategy or changing a frozen episode. The private `SOURCE_COVERAGE_REQUEST_B64` request fixes the producer run, source revision, archive identities, checksums, download budget and decision-time policy before acquisition. It is retained inside the encrypted result bundle. Public pull requests and workflow output contain generic tooling and synthetic checks only.

Acquisition is restricted to the configured public source repository. Download URLs are constructed from validated release and archive names. Selected archives must match their registered identities, sizes and checksums; failures do not trigger replacement with a different sample. Compressed bytes, decompressed bytes, line lengths and record counts are bounded. Existing sealed-delivery and journal-persistence tooling handles the results.

The audit reports its sampled time range and market-bucket coverage. For each market bucket in that range, it first chooses a time anchor under the registered policy, then assesses field availability and causal timestamp relationships. Freshness is measured at the decision cutoff, with all source clocks also required to be no later than the selected snapshot. Explicit reference-source identity is checked independently of producer-derived rule names. It does not move a bad anchor to a better observation. Opening-reference availability, published reference-price consistency, spot-volatility availability are reported separately from future official labels. Missing anchors, missing labels and boundary coverage remain visible in the denominators.

Producer validity flags and a successful capture job are inputs to inspect, not independent certification. Legacy published TWAP snapshots do not prove authenticated stream completeness or the integral over the known part of a future settlement window. Spot volatility is not automatically oracle uncertainty. Scheduled end, published outcome, redeemability and reusable cash are distinct observations; this audit creates no cash receipt or live fill.

Only encrypted evidence, diagnostics and generic delivery status are uploaded. The frozen model and original research/data-processing files are unchanged. Use the synthetic `test_settlement_source_coverage.py`, `test_source_coverage_runner.py` and existing journal tests for local validation; real archive inspection runs in the manual cloud workflow.
