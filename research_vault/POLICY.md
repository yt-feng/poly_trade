# Bounded continuous research policy

Only generic workflow rules are public. All new per-run hypotheses, proprietary findings, experiment outputs, selected wallet cohorts and work summaries must be sealed before publication. No valid independently checked public recipient means NO publication of private findings; never fall back to plaintext or a PIN-only cipher.

Improve authorized research methods and expected user outcomes, not the agent's own resources. No unlimited-compute or guaranteed-growth claims. Research can be paused. Preserve existing account and order boundaries; no automatic live trading, credential collection, fund movement or risk escalation.

At each scheduled wake: read current versions and new-data watermarks; distinguish revised old inputs from unseen observations; avoid rerunning unchanged samples to hunt a winner. Keep work summaries and factual findings, not hidden reasoning traces. Store source versions, hashes and data-availability times inside the sealed journal. Record costs, missing observations and all failed attempts.

Fast experiments are encouraged as separate discovery jobs with a declared compute/time budget. Preregister each candidate family, comparisons, thresholds and review checkpoints before the relevant test. Do not optimize on the confirmatory set. Reusing or changing a test set creates a new version and does not make old evidence independent. Apply time-based purging, sensitivity analysis and multiplicity controls appropriate to the data; merely importing a statistical library does not establish its assumptions.

Separate signal skill, execution, exit policy and capital allocation. Evaluate net return distribution and available-cash turnover, not win rate, nominal odds or signal frequency alone. Keep the existing exit and cash-reconciliation constraints until an independently evaluated change justifies revision. Do not increase order sizes to repair an unprofitable signal. Discrete position planning must include zero exposure.

Public-wallet research uses pseudonymous public on-chain evidence only. Freeze selection before evaluation; today's leaderboard cannot select yesterday's winners for a hindsight backtest. Distinguish share count from cash value, fills from mint/merge/transfer events, maker from taker evidence, and event time from actual first observation time. Never infer a wallet's unobserved cancellations or off-platform hedges from fills alone. The supplied-observation contract does not authenticate chain data or issue a trade recommendation.

Root-cause notes distinguish deterministic reproduced software defects, controlled replay counterfactuals, observational association and unidentified causes. A successful example is not a causal effect. Falsification and stopping a failed line are valid progress; code/test/report counts are not profits.

Only sufficiently supported candidates advance to human research review. Execution readiness and funding authorization are separate and cannot be enabled by a test pass. No new empirical findings or identifying cohorts go into public Actions output or PR text. Existing plaintext history is a known disclosure, not cured by this policy.
